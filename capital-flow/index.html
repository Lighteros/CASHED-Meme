Capital flow

Creator fees from pons are claimed on a schedule, split 80/20 at that moment, and credited to the creator the token named, on X, Spotify, Twitch, GitHub or fomo. The creator signs in with that account and withdraws in dollars; a fomo trader is paid straight to their wallet.

pons curve and pool
Every trade pays a fee in the launch's pairing asset. The creator's share accrues on the curve, then on the Uniswap v4 hook after graduation.
Fee escrow
pons sweeps fees into its escrow under the vault's address. Anyone can trigger the next step.
Cashed claims
Fees are claimed per handle and split on the spot: 80% to the recipient, 20% to the treasury. Every claim is keyed to its transaction.
Credited to the creator
The recipient share sits in the creator's own fee address, claimable only by the account the token named.
Withdrawn in dollars
The creator signs in on X, Spotify, Twitch or GitHub and withdraws as USDG in one transaction, gas paid by Cashed; a fomo trader receives the USDG in the wallet they trade with, no sign-in. X creators will also be able to receive through 𝕏 Money once it is live.
