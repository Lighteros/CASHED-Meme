How it works
  1. 1Name a creatorLaunch on pons and point the creator fees at an account on X, Spotify, Twitch, GitHub or fomo. It does not need to know.
  2. 2Cashed claimsFees are claimed on a schedule and split on chain: 80% to the creator, 20% to the protocol.
  3. 3Paid in dollarsThe creator signs in with that account and withdraws the share as USDG, gas paid by us.
